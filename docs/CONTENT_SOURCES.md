# Product content sources and review status

The catalog in `content/products.ts` is static preview data, not a database seed or a live inventory source. Product prices, sizes, ingredients, and the combo's free-delivery offer were supplied by the owner on 13 November 2026. Product names were transcribed from the owner's supplied images. The owner subsequently confirmed starting stock of 30 units each for the oil, shampoo, and lotion. Their `initialStock` and preview `stock` fields record that starting snapshot; checkout must use current database inventory.

The duo has no independent inventory: its `stock` and `initialStock` are `null`, and `bundleComponents` references one oil and one shampoo. Future inventory handling must derive bundle availability from component stock and reserve/decrement those components, so the same bottles cannot be sold twice.

Prices are stored as integer Pakistani paisa, where 100 paisa equals Rs. 1. The oil is Rs. 749, shampoo Rs. 1,249, lotion Rs. 340, and duo Rs. 2,000. The two individual hair products total Rs. 1,998, so the duo must not be advertised as a product-price discount. Its confirmed offer is free delivery. The owner confirmed that an order containing any duo receives free delivery for the whole order, including additional products. Normal delivery is Rs. 250 across Pakistan.

## Draft descriptions and usage

The owner explicitly requested standard descriptions and usage guidance. The descriptions and directions in the preview catalog are assistant-written drafts using the supplied product purposes and ingredients. Each item has `contentStatus: "draft"`; the manufacturer should check the instructions against the finished formulation and label before publication. No application duration, frequency, suitability for a skin or hair type, or product performance result has been invented.

The supplied ingredient lists are not confirmed complete ingredient declarations or INCI lists. Display them as **Key ingredients** until the owner supplies the full formulation list. Ingredient names have been translated or spelling-normalized, not used to infer concentrations, extraction methods, or botanical species in the products.
