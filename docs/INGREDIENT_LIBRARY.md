## Content scope and references

The 11 items and their product mapping come from the owner's ingredient list. These are key ingredients, not complete INCI lists. Glycerin's origin and the lotion wax type have not been supplied. The lotion group is not labelled entirely botanical or natural.

Short benefit descriptions explain general cosmetic uses or traditional roles. They do not assert clinical efficacy for these finished products, concentrations, extract types, certifications, safety for all users, hair regrowth or dandruff treatment. Text remains subject to the owner's formulation and label review.

- [COSMILE: amla fruit powder](https://cosmileeurope.eu/inci/detail/5227/emblica-officinalis-fruit-powder/) supports the general conditioning role. This does not establish the form used in these products.
- [COSMILE: soapnut fruit/pericarp extract](https://cosmileeurope.eu/pl/inci/szczegoly/19784/sapindus-mukorossi-fruit-pericarp-extract/) lists cleansing and surfactant functions.
- [Primary herbal shampoo formulation study](https://www.ijcpi.com/article/313/public) describes traditional saponin-rich hair-wash ingredients including shikakai. Only the general cleansing role is used here, not efficacy claims about this store's shampoo.
- [Coconut oil hair study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12715094/) informs the general hair-care context. The card uses modest lubrication/friction language, not a quantified benefit or product-specific study claim.
- [COSMILE: rosemary leaf extract](https://cosmileeurope.eu/inci/detail/13860/rosmarinus-officinalis-leaf-extract/) describes fragrance, conditioning and refreshing cosmetic uses.
- [Hibiscus ingredient context](https://cosmileeurope.eu/inci/detail/29718/hydrolyzed-hibiscus-rosa-sinensis-flower-extract/) describes one specific preparation; it is not evidence that the owner's shampoo uses hydrolysed extract. The card describes traditional conditioning use only.
- [Linseed extract technical information](https://pub-mediabox-storage.rxweb-prd.com/exhibitor/products/exh-bad7035c-b03c-423c-9ade-7b3eb90f0bd0/product-documents/pro-66ea6e6a-31f9-43b9-9f47-bc36a000c2f2/6dc3a6d9-fdab-4221-bd4f-bdf1c76f7d99.pdf) explains mucilage and its coating properties. No supplier-specific efficacy figures are used.
- [Fenugreek supplier technical sheet](https://materie-prime.farmalabor.it/schede/001057.PDF) identifies mucilage among seed constituents. The copy describes traditional use, not a trial result.
- [Cosmetics Info: glycerin](https://www.cosmeticsinfo.org/ingredient/glycerin/) explains its humectant role.
- [Cosmetics Info: petrolatum](https://www.cosmeticsinfo.org/ingredient/petrolatum/) explains moisture retention.
